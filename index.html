● Darkroom — Privacy Policy

Published by Halvorsen Elektromekk · Last updated 24 September 2026

Summary

Darkroom itself does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob, which does collect some data — see Advertising below. Buying “Remove ads” turns the ads off.

What stays on your device

Your developing log, queued reels, recipes, notes, chemistry records, owned-equipment ticks and settings are saved only in the app’s private storage on your phone. They are included in your phone’s own Android backup if you have it turned on (handled by Google under your Google account, not by us).

Photos

Photos you add to the log are resized and stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. They are never uploaded. They’re included in your phone’s own Android backup if you have it turned on.

Notifications and alarms

If you allow notifications, the app shows timer alerts, a home-screen widget and a quick settings tile while a timer runs. It uses exact alarms only to time those alerts; nothing is sent anywhere.

Feedback

The Feedback screen only prepares an email in your own email app. Nothing is sent unless you press send there. If you leave “Include app version and device details” ticked, the email includes the app version, phone model, Android version, language and screen size — and nothing else.

Files

When you use Export log or Save supplies list, the file is saved only where you choose. Import log reads only the file you pick.

Permissions

The app requests vibration and notifications (for timer cues), exact alarms (so cues fire on time with the screen locked), and internet access (only for the banner ad and Google Play). It does not request location, camera, contacts or storage permissions — photos are picked with Android’s own file picker, which only shares the photo you choose.

Advertising

The banner ad is provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, under Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you’re asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time under Menu → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Purchases and tips

“Remove ads” and optional tips are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details and receipt; Darkroom never sees your name, card or account details. The app only stores, on your device, whether Remove ads has been bought (which removes the banner and unlocks red darkroom mode) and that a tip was made, so it can say thank you.

Google Play features

The app uses Google Play’s In-App Updates and In-App Review features. These run through the Google Play Store app under Google’s privacy policy. Darkroom receives no personal data from them.

Links

Video links in Learn open YouTube in your browser or the YouTube app, which have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Everything Darkroom stores is on your phone. Clear log removes the log and its photos, and uninstalling the app removes everything. To remove ad-related data, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings, or change your choice under Menu → Ad privacy choices.

Children

Darkroom is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
